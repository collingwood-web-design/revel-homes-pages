Budget

Establishing a budget early in the design process is the single best predictor of a successful project. We work with our clients to set a realistic budget first, then shape the design to meet it, so the home you fall in love with on paper is the home you can actually afford to build.

To make that first conversation easier, we built the custom home build calculator below. Tell it about the home you have in mind: your municipality, the square footage, one storey or two, the garage, whether the basement gets finished, and the level of finish you want in the spaces that matter most — including the kitchen. It combines those choices with regional construction cost data and gives you a realistic starting range in about a minute.

More importantly, it shows you how each decision moves the number. Add a second storey and watch the range shift. Step the kitchen up from standard to premium, finish the basement now instead of later, or compare municipalities with different development charges. That is what budgeting a dream home really looks like: seeing what each part of the design and build costs before you commit to it, and deciding where the money matters most to you.

The result is an educated starting point, not a quote. When you have a range you like, send it to us and we will walk you through the real numbers for your lot.
